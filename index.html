Before you bid

  • Read the full auction notice and reserve price
  • Verify the title and any encumbrances
  • Confirm possession status: physical or symbolic
  • Check dues: electricity, water, society, tax
  • Check JDA / land-use status of the plot
  • Arrange EMD and the balance payment timeline

How an auction purchase works

  1. Shortlist. We find auction properties that fit your budget and area.
  2. Check. Title, dues, possession and JDA status are reviewed before you commit.
  3. Bid. Register, deposit EMD and bid within the limit you set.
  4. Pay. Complete the balance payment within the bank's deadline.
  5. Register. Get the sale certificate, registry and mutation done.

Common questions

Is buying a bank auction property safe?

It can be, when the title, dues and possession are checked first. Auction properties are sold as-is, so the checks before bidding matter most.

Do you help with properties that lack JDA approval?

Yes. We review the status and explain the options, such as regularisation or conversion, before you decide.

Which areas do you cover?

Jaipur and the surrounding region. Tell us the locality you have in mind.
